What is eDPI?

eDPI, or effective DPI, is your mouse DPI multiplied by your in-game sensitivity.

It is useful for comparing sensitivity settings within the same game.

What is cm/360°?

cm/360° represents the approximate physical mouse distance required to rotate your character 360 degrees.

It is especially useful when comparing sensitivity across different games.

How does conversion work?

The converter compares the mouse-input rotation scale of the two games and calculates the target sensitivity required to maintain the same physical 360° movement.

Is the converted sensitivity guaranteed to feel identical?

No. A mathematically equivalent 360° distance does not guarantee identical aiming feel.

FOV, weapon mechanics, ADS systems, acceleration, animation and other game-specific mechanics can affect how sensitivity feels.

Sensitivity Converter FAQ

What DPI should I use?

There is no universally correct DPI. 400, 800 and 1600 are common choices, but your overall sensitivity is what determines your physical mouse movement.

Does FOV affect cm/360°?

No. FOV changes how much of the game world you can see, but it does not change the physical mouse movement required for a 360° camera rotation.

Why is my converted sensitivity different from another calculator?

Different calculators can use different game input constants, conversion methods or assumptions. The exact implementation used by a particular game also matters.

Does this convert ADS sensitivity?

The primary conversion is for general mouse rotational sensitivity. ADS and scoped sensitivities can use game-specific multipliers and should be treated separately.

Does higher cm/360° mean slower sensitivity?

Yes. A larger cm/360° value means you need to move your mouse farther to complete a 360° turn.

Can I share my conversion?

Yes. The Share button creates a URL containing your selected games, sensitivity and DPI. Someone opening that URL can see the same conversion settings.

Can I use this on my phone?

Yes. The converter is fully responsive and works on desktop, tablet and mobile screens.
